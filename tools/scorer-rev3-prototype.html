What tightened vs the approved mockup

Change
The card turned vertical. Two columns (Out | In) like a paper scorecard instead of two rows of nine — every hole is now a ~190×52px target. Nine-across on a normal phone maths out to ~37px cells, under the pressure test's own 44px floor.
Easier
Par lives in every cell — hole, par, yards at a glance, no header lookup. The pad's par button is the biggest thing on it; most taps are one obvious tap.
Easier
The next empty hole breathes. A soft pulse suggests where you likely are — a hint only, never a selection, so shotgun starts stay untouched.
Easier
The pad is a bottom sheet in thumb reach, with the running score pinned on top the whole time. Tap hole, tap number, done — the cell stamps in as the receipt.
Refined
The site's own face. Bodoni numerals, Jost-family labels, pine and brass — it reads as a page of the GFY site, not a bolt-on app. All states carry a mark plus color, nothing under 13px except decorative sub-labels.
Refined
Conflicts read like a ruling, not an error. Two numbers, two buttons, plain words. Everything else about the write path is untouched §18: sheet-answered confirms, offline-first, no-clobber.
