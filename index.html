Committee decisions, final

Local Rules

The USGA governs the rest. These override it.

  1. Two days, two rounds, one course. No substitutions, no early exits, no working the weekend.
  2. Two-day scramble, gross score. Gross wins The Bird and keeps it for a year.
  3. One mulligan per team per nine. Call it before the swing or it never happened.
  4. Ball in the pines is playable. Ball in the creek is a drop and a story.
  5. Phones stay in the cart. A work call costs you a stroke and a round back at the lodge.
  6. Gimmes are inside the leather and inside the leather only. The leather is not your driver.
  7. The Calcutta is bid Friday after the draw. Winning bids are due to the pot before Sunday's first tee.
  8. Anyone who complains about the pace of play gets told the name of the tournament.
